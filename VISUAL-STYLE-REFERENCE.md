# Org Chart Visual Style Reference

Use these IDs as shorthand for future design updates. They describe the established page treatments and are reference names, not CSS classes.

## Team and section headings

| ID | Reference | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **T1** | Team title banner, such as “APJC WTG Marketing” | Wide, rounded banner with left-aligned bold dark text, a pale blue-to-warm-neutral gradient, fine light border, and soft shadow. No blue stripe on the left. Used between organization-wide leadership and the team leader. |
| **T2** | Focus-area heading, such as “Regional Product & Solutions Marketing” | Rounded, subtly raised heading card with bold dark text and a pale blue-to-warm-neutral gradient. Long headings wrap at spaces. No blue stripe on the left. |
| **T3** | Nested team heading, such as “Content & Campaigns” | Smaller rounded heading card with bold dark text, a warm neutral fill, fine light border, and soft shadow. No gradient accent or blue stripe. |

## Person cards

| ID | Reference | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **C1** | Organization leader, such as Saleema Syed | Primary wide leadership card. Circular grayscale portrait, typically 66 px; name 14 pt, bold dark navy (#202938); title and portfolio line 12 pt, muted slate (#687386–#717c8c); 9 pt status badge. The left status stripe and badge follow the person’s status color. |
| **C2** | Leadership support, such as Kendys Carrizo | Compact support-column card. Circular grayscale portrait, typically 42 px; name 11 pt, bold dark navy; title 10 pt, muted slate; 9 pt status badge; right-side chevron. The left status stripe and badge follow the person’s status color. |
| **C3** | Team leader, such as Julie Berg or Blossom Furtado | Full-width primary card below the T1 banner. Circular grayscale portrait, typically 54–66 px depending on page component; prominent bold dark navy name, muted slate title, status badge, and right-side chevron where the card opens a profile. The left status stripe and badge follow the person’s status color. |
| **C4** | Focus-area person, such as Bailee Marcum or Aiko Yasuda | Standard horizontal person card. Circular grayscale portrait, typically 54 px; 12 pt bold dark navy name; 10.5 pt muted slate title; 9 pt status badge. The left status stripe and badge follow the person’s status color. |

## Shared text and photo rules

- Names, titles, headings, and status labels wrap at spaces. Never split a word in the middle or hyphenate it to fit; move the whole word to the next line.
- Keep portraits circular and grayscale, with consistent sizing within each card type and framing that shows the full face.
- Preserve the warm off-white page background, dark navy names and headings, muted slate supporting text, soft shadows, rounded corners, and blue FTE accents.
- Keep heading levels distinct: T1 names the team, T2 names a focus area, and T3 names a nested team.
